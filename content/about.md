## What is For&Chips?

**For&Chips** is an informal community blog about digital forensics.

It started from a simple frustration: keep redoing the same research, losing track of findings, and reinventing the wheel every few months. So a group of people who love chips — the crunchy kind and the silicon kind — decided to put it all in one place.

No formal agenda, no editorial board. Just a shared space to document research, publish write-ups, and avoid forgetting what we already figured out.
